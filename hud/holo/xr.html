Hands
  • PINCH (thumb tip to index tip) near the hologram: grab it and move it in 3D.
  • TWO-HAND PINCH: move your hands apart or together to scale it, turn them to rotate it.
  • PULL BOTH PINCHING HANDS APART FAST: it explodes into its named parts.
  • Exploded: PINCH A PART to pull it out; HOLD ABOUT A SECOND for its name and size.
  • CLAP (palms meet fast): it assembles again.
  • POKE a name on the shelf at its left to swap the model.
